Questions families ask

Before you sign

What does the 6 month waiting period mean?

Cover for death from natural causes starts once you have paid six full monthly premiums. Accidental death is covered from the first premium. Come to the office and we will explain exactly where your plan stands.

Who can I put on my plan?

Yourself as the main member plus your dependants, up to the number on your plan. Depending on the plan the limit is 5, 9 or 13 additional members. Members must be within the age limit shown on the plan.

What happens if I miss a month?

Speak to the office before it becomes two months. A lapsed plan can usually be reinstated, but the waiting period may start again. Bring your book to 09 Union Street.

Where do I pay?

At the office in Union Street, or with your EasyPay card at any Shoprite, Checkers, Boxer, Spar, Pick n Pay or Pep till. Keep your slip and have your book stamped at the office.

Can I move to a bigger plan later?

Yes. Come to the office and we will move you up. A new waiting period may apply to the difference in cover.
